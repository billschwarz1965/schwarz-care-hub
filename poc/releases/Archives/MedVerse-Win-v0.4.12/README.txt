MedVerse Scientific Intelligence Ecosystem v0.4 — Portable Edition
====================================================================

NO INSTALLATION REQUIRED — NO ADMIN RIGHTS NEEDED

Getting Started:
  1. Extract this entire folder to any location on your PC
     (Desktop, Documents, Downloads — anywhere you have write access)
  2. Double-click "Launch MedVerse.bat" or "MedVerse Operating System.exe"

That's it. The app runs entirely from this folder.

What's Inside:
  - 10 AI-powered modules for MSLs, HCPs, patients, and medical affairs
  - Interactive demos with narrated walkthroughs
  - Master Demo page with one-click Play All mode

Modules:
  - MSL Copilot (13 agents)
  - Medical Concierge (12 agents)
  - HCP Concierge
  - Patient Concierge (11 agents)
  - Orion Intelligence
  - Disease Navigator
  - Literature Intelligence
  - Congress Intelligence
  - Agent Ecosystem
  - Power Agents

To Uninstall:
  Just delete this folder. Nothing is written to the registry or system folders.
