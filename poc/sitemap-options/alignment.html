Which sitemap fits poc-external's content model?

Once medical.campus.sanofi is built, its educational content becomes a data source poc-external's Disease Navigator (and possibly HCP Concierge) pulls from. This page compares the three sitemap options against what poc-external's code actually needs — not the marketing copy on each option's landing page.

RECOMMENDATION

Option 2 (TA-nested global) is the best structural fit. It has the same local disease taxonomy as the other two, plus a stable, country-agnostic URL (/immunology) poc-external can point to even before a non-US market exists. Option 3 is the weakest fit — its rebuild against the real sitemap workbook proved that at least one Therapeutic Area's shape genuinely changes by market, and poc-external has no locale logic anywhere today to handle that.
