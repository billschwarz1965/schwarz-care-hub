About MedVerse

The Scientific Intelligence Ecosystem for Sanofi Medical Affairs

Our Vision

MedVerse is Sanofi's AI-powered Scientific Intelligence Ecosystem — a unified platform that transforms how Medical Affairs, healthcare professionals, patients, and caregivers access and interact with scientific knowledge. By bringing together 50+ specialized AI agents, MedVerse serves as the intelligent gateway between field insights and enterprise intelligence.

What MedVerse Does

  • Empowers MSLs with real-time intelligence for HCP engagements
  • Provides HCPs with evidence-based clinical decision support
  • Delivers patient-friendly health information and treatment guidance
  • Supports caregivers with accessible educational resources
  • Synthesizes scientific literature, congress intelligence, and regulatory updates
  • Generates actionable signals from field interactions via Orion integration

Platform Architecture

MedVerse operates as a multi-module ecosystem with specialized concierge experiences for each audience. AI agents are purpose-built for specific tasks — from medical information requests and publication planning to disease state education and competitive intelligence. The platform features voice search, narrated demos, and light/dark mode across all modules.
