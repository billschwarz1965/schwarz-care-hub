About Patient Concierge

A standalone build of MedVerse's patient- and caregiver-facing companion — plain-language health information, day-to-day tracking tools, and help finding support, all in one place.

What This Build Is

This edition ships one module: Patient Concierge. It is the patient- and caregiver-facing companion from the full MedVerse platform, on its own — no HCP tooling, no Medical Affairs or MSL modules, and no Agent Ecosystem view. What's here is everything a patient or caregiver needs to understand a condition, manage day-to-day treatment, and get help when it's time to talk to a doctor.

Every answer is written in plain language, is not a substitute for medical advice, and always points back to a healthcare provider for anything that needs one.

What You Can Do Here

  • Look up a medication for dosing, side effects, interactions, and storage — in plain language
  • Check symptoms and get guidance on urgency and next steps
  • Explore treatment options for your condition and treatment stage
  • Find clinical trials you may be eligible for
  • Find patient assistance programs, copay cards, and insurance support
  • Build a personalized question list before a doctor visit
  • Log and track medication side effects over time
  • Learn about a health condition — causes, symptoms, and living well with a diagnosis
  • Get resources and support as a family caregiver
  • Track daily symptoms, mood, energy, and adherence in a wellness journal

Part of a Wider Platform

Patient Concierge is one module of MedVerse. The full platform also includes a concierge experience for healthcare professionals, dedicated field tooling for MSLs and Medical Affairs, an intelligence layer for disease navigation, literature, and medical congresses, and an Agent Ecosystem explorer for the architecture behind all of it. Those modules are not part of this build.
