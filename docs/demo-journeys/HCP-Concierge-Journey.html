HCP Concierge Journey

A healthcare professional looking for clinical decision support. They start with a spoken or typed question, get routed to the right agent, and can move on to field contact, safety checks and research without leaving the concierge.

12agents
6HCP-specific
5shared with other personas
1AI chat assistant
HCP-specific agent Shared across personas Always-on chat

Entry

  1. 1
    Voice Search ENTRY
    Speak a clinical question hands-free. It is auto-routed to the right agent.

Clinical decision support

  1. 2
    Clinical Q&A CORE
    Evidence-based answers with citations from Sanofi medical content. Also generates an interaction signal.
  2. 3
    Patient Navigator CORE
    Builds a personalized care pathway from age, diagnosis, comorbidities and prior treatments.
  3. 4
    Trial Matching Agent SHARED
    Matches the patient to eligible Sanofi trials by indication, age, region and biologic status.

Field & safety

  1. 5
    MSL Connect CORE
    Finds the regional MSL by therapeutic area, then requests a meeting, email or call.
  2. 6
    Ingredient Safety CORE
    Cross-references excipients against patient allergies, with latex and polysorbate alerts.
  3. 7
    Temperature Stability CORE
    Storage guidance, travel conditions and cold-chain requirements for medications.

Research & intelligence

  1. 8
    Literature Intelligence SHARED
    Searches PubMed for clinical evidence, long-term safety and head-to-head data.
  2. 9
    Literature Scout SHARED
    Proactive alerts for new publications that match the HCP's clinical interests.
  3. 10
    Disease Navigator SHARED
    Cross-therapeutic-area disease landscape: pathways, biomarkers and treatment options.
  4. 11
    Congress Intelligence SHARED
    Medical congress presentations, posters and key highlights.

Always available

  1. 12
    HCP Concierge Assistant AI CHAT
    Conversational assistant spanning all 11 agents, for any question at any time.
