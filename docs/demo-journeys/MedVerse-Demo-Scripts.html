More HCP use cases to demo

These come from the suggested questions library under the Ask MedVerse search bar (poc/src/ask-prompts.js). The grey note says what each question is meant to prove in front of an audience.

Worth demoing deliberately

These show the system declining to invent an answer.

  • "Who is my MSL for dermatology?" MSL Connect
  • "Is Dupixent safe for a patient with a polysorbate allergy?" Ingredient Safety
  • "Dupixent was left out of the fridge overnight, is it still usable?" Temperature Stability
  • "How do I request compassionate use for an unapproved medicine?" Medical Information plus all four HCP access pathways (MAP, iEnvision portal, PTA)

Trials and pipeline

  • "What trials are recruiting for atopic dermatitis?" 5 real studies with NCT IDs, phase, enrollment, sites
  • "What clinical trials are available for cancer?" 5 oncology trials plus ASCO / AACR / ASH / COMy
  • "What is in the pipeline for Gaucher disease?" venglustat (Gaucher type 3) plus 6 RDU resources

The honest-failure demo

The behaviour that makes everything else trustworthy: no nearest-document guess.

  • "Treatment options for pancreatic cancer" Answers "no indexed clinical evidence" rather than serving the nearest document
