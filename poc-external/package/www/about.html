About MedVerse

Sanofi's Scientific Intelligence Ecosystem for Healthcare Professionals

Our Vision

MedVerse is Sanofi's AI-powered Scientific Intelligence Ecosystem — a unified platform that transforms how healthcare professionals access and interact with scientific knowledge. Ask a question in plain language and get an evidence-based answer with citations you can open and check for yourself.

This build is the external-audience edition. Sanofi's internal field-medical and medical affairs tooling is part of the same platform, but is not included here.

What MedVerse Does

  • Ask MedVerse — one search box that classifies any question and routes it to whichever agents can answer it
  • Provides HCPs with evidence-based clinical decision support, fully cited
  • Matches HCPs' patients to eligible clinical trials
  • Synthesizes scientific literature and congress data from live sources
  • Connects HCPs to a regional medical science liaison on request

Platform Architecture

MedVerse operates as a multi-module ecosystem with a purpose-built concierge experience for each audience. AI agents are built for specific tasks — from clinical Q&A and trial matching to disease state education and congress coverage — and every output passes through a governance layer before it reaches you. The platform features voice search, narrated demos, and light/dark mode across all modules.

Connected Content Sources

Answers are not limited to internal clinical documents. MedVerse indexes real educational content published across Sanofi's medical properties, so a response can point an HCP to the podcast, video, infographic, or article that covers the topic — and to where it lives.

  • ADVENT — type 2 inflammation education (Sanofi & Regeneron): podcasts, expert videos, infographics
  • Rare Diseases University — learning modules across Fabry, Gaucher, ASMD, MPS I, and hemophilia
  • BR1DGE — presymptomatic type 1 diabetes education
  • Sanofi Congress Library — dated congress records across immunology, neurology, transplant, and rare blood disorders
  • Sanofi Rare Diseases Medical — diagnostic and biomarker guidance, clinical monographs, symposium recordings
  • Sanofi Payer Medical — value propositions and data decks for healthcare decision makers
  • Sanofi Medical Information — the approved medical information database and request pathway
  • Sanofi Clinical Trials — the trial finder and therapeutic-area trial education

Every surfaced resource keeps its content type, source program, and a link back to the original page, so the location of the content is always visible rather than paraphrased away.
